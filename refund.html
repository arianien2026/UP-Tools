Refund Policy / 退款政策

EnSound UP 購買相關處理 / EnSound UP purchase support

此為依目前產品規劃撰寫的公開草案，正式上線前仍須檢視。
This is a draft based on the current product plan and requires review before launch.

購買前體驗 / Try before purchasing

EnSound UP 提供免費練習，讓使用者在購買前評估產品。

EnSound UP offers free practice so users can evaluate the product before purchasing.

退款申請 / Refund requests

購買後 7 天內，如遇經核實的重複扣款、付款成功但 Full Access 未正確啟用、經合理排除仍未解決的重大技術故障，或其他經核實的付款或產品技術問題，可寄信至 uptools.support@gmail.com 申請退款處理。

Within 7 days of purchase, contact uptools.support@gmail.com to request refund handling for verified duplicate charges, successful payments without correct Full Access activation, material technical failures unresolved after reasonable troubleshooting, or other verified payment or product technical failures.

退款處理時間 / Processing

退款若獲核准，UP Tools 將透過金流服務商發起退款。實際入帳時間取決於金流服務商、收單銀行、發卡機構或相關金融機構。

If a refund is approved, UP Tools initiates it through the payment provider. Actual posting time depends on the provider, acquiring bank, card issuer, or relevant financial institution.

消費者權利 / Consumer rights

本退款政策不限制或取代消費者依適用法律享有的權利。

This Refund Policy does not limit or replace consumer rights provided by applicable law.
